# Support

Start with the [installation guide](docs/installation.md), [configuration reference](docs/configuration.md), and [troubleshooting guide](docs/troubleshooting.md).
[Compatibility](docs/compatibility.md) describes tested versions and known limits.

Search [existing issues](https://github.com/jerriclynsjohn/tmux-agent-pulse/issues) before opening a new one.
Choose the form that fits:

- [Question](https://github.com/jerriclynsjohn/tmux-agent-pulse/issues/new?template=question.yml) for help with a setup or workflow.
- [Bug report](https://github.com/jerriclynsjohn/tmux-agent-pulse/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) for unexpected behavior, installation problems, or unclear instructions.
- [Feature request](https://github.com/jerriclynsjohn/tmux-agent-pulse/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) for a missing capability or a proposed workflow.

Describe what you want to do and where you get stuck.

Include the plugin commit or tag, your tmux and Python versions, the provider version, and the smallest relevant configuration.
Share only relevant diagnostic output after removing private details.
You don't need to upload conversation transcripts or your full configuration.

Report suspected vulnerabilities through the private route in [Security](SECURITY.md).
See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) to propose a fix.

The maintainer answers through this repository as time allows.
There is no guaranteed response time.
